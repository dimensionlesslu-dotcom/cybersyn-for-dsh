## Project-local policies

- L1-L4 routing is a control-strength policy, not a universal taxonomy.
- A/B/C/D deviations mean execution, measurement, environment, and interaction; records may carry multiple labels.
- L4 defaults to at least three active structural models. The number is configurable and is not derived from a theorem.
- J-workspace defaults to six items. Capacity overflow fails visibly rather than silently evicting a potentially important fact.
- Human prompt edits invalidate evidence for the edited task and all graph descendants. This is a conservative assurance rule, not a claim about every possible semantic impact.
- Subagent-role budgets are L1=0, L2=1, L3=2, and L4=3. The numbers are cost/coverage policies inherited from the source Skill, not an optimality theorem.
- Operational health and external effectiveness are reported separately. Missing measurements remain `unmeasured`; no-error execution alone is never evidence of quality.
- One failed or cancelled audit attempt may be retried once. The plugin permits and records that transition but never launches the retry itself.
